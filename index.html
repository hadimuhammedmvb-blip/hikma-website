ABOUT US

Welcome to HIKMA, the H2 Class Union of Liwa Ul Huda Islamic College, a college under the Samastha National Education Council (SNEC), affiliated with Samastha Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama.

HIKMA stands as a platform for inspiring young minds, nurturing talents, and empowering students to achieve their full potential. Guided by the noble ideals of Ahlussunnati Wal Jama'ah, we strive to develop students' intellectual, creative, and moral abilities through meaningful activities, inspiring speeches, literary contributions, and insightful guest talks.

Our mission is to encourage every student to discover their strengths, express their ideas, enhance their knowledge, and grow into confident, responsible, and talented individuals. Through unity, creativity, knowledge, and dedication, HIKMA aims to create a vibrant learning community where every talent is valued and every mind is inspired.
